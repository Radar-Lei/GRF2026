# Project Data

This research project will generate multiple datasets of significant retention value for future research in transport equity, gender studies, and computational social science. The primary dataset will be a comprehensive global multilingual social media corpus spanning three years, containing millions of posts from social media platforms related to metro experiences across diverse cities, with associated geolocation data, temporal metadata, and demographic inference results through multi-modal gender attribution techniques. The project will also produce a specialized sentiment analysis training dataset with domain-specific annotations for gendered transit experiences, including expert-labeled categories for safety concerns, harassment incidents, and comfort perceptions across different cultural contexts. Additionally, innovative LLM-assisted focus group data will be collected from strategically selected cities to address digital representativeness biases, incorporating voice-to-text transcriptions, multilingual translations, and cultural context interpretations. These datasets will enable comparative analysis of gendered mobility barriers across cities, support evidence-based policy interventions dashboards, and establish new standards for social media analytics in transport research.
